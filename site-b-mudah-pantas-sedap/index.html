Three easy dinners, one pack each. Tap a recipe for the steps.

20min

with Pes Asam Pedas

Asam pedas ikan tenggiri

Sour, fiery and on the table before the rice is done.

  1. Fry one pack of Pes Asam Pedas in 2 tbsp oil for 2 minutes.
  2. Add 2 cups of water and bring it to the boil.
  3. Add 4 to 5 slices of ikan tenggiri, some bendi and a tomato.
  4. Simmer for 8 to 10 minutes, add daun kesum, and serve with rice.
45min

with Pes Rendang

Rendang daging

The Raya favourite, without a whole day at the stove.

  1. Fry one pack of Pes Rendang for 3 minutes until it darkens.
  2. Add 1 kg of beef, cut into cubes, and stir to coat.
  3. Pour in 200 ml of santan and simmer, stirring often.
  4. Cook for 40 to 45 minutes until the gravy is thick and dark.
20min

with Pes Mi Bandung

Mi bandung Muar

Thick, savoury gravy with prawns, beef and a soft egg.

  1. Fry one pack of Pes Mi Bandung for 2 minutes.
  2. Add 3 cups of water, a handful of prawns and sliced beef.
  3. Crack in an egg and stir gently as it sets.
  4. Pour over blanched yellow mee and top with sawi.
